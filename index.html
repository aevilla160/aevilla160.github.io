Hello!

I'm Alex, I am an incoming ECE Ph.D. student at the University of Florida.

View CV ↗

Previously, I completed my B.S. in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of California, Merced. I also obtained a minor in Applied Mathematics.

I am broadly interested in high performance and parallel computing with applications to Deep Learning and Computational Physics. More specifically I am interested in designing efficient collective communication primitives for emerging workloads, tensor optimizations, and scalable scientific machine learning.

My research has been supported by Los Alamos National Laboratory, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and Google-CASHI. See my research interests here

Research Interests

  • Collective communication (MPI, NCCL): topology-aware algorithms and optimizations for emerging and irregular workloads (SciML, Quantum).
  • Efficient Parallel Algorithms and Architectures: optimization techniques for GPUs and parallel algorithms.
  • Physics-informed ML & neural operators: scalability, stability, and robustness at extreme scale.
  • Scalable parallel frameworks for scientific computing.

I am inspired and motivated through problems that arise in Deep Learning and Computational Physics applications.
