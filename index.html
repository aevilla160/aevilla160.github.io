About Me

I'm an undergraduate Computer Science & Engineering student at UC Merced (Class of 2026). Driven by curiosity, I thrive on crafting efficient HPC solutions to tackle real-world scientific challenges.

I am passionate about collective communication protocols (MPI, NCCL), GPU offloading and optimization, data parallelism alternatives and performance analysis

I am particularly interested in collective communication operation optimizations for deep learning and scientific computing. Furthermore, I am interested in the scalability and robustness of neural operators (for PDE learning) and physics informed machine learning.

Research Interests

My research focuses on high-performance computing (HPC) techniques, particularly in the areas of:
